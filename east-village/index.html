Welcome to East Village, a luxury apartment community in the heart of St. Albert. With 188 thoughtfully designed one and two bedroom apartments, this modern neighbourhood blends luxury, comfort, and convenience to create a place you’ll be proud to call home.

Inside, you’ll find layouts thoughtfully designed for everyday living, paired with perks and amenities that make life here effortless.  Relax on the rooftop terrace or by the fire pits, catch a movie in the private theatre, host friends in the celebration lounge, or stay active in the fully equipped fitness centre. Secure keyless entry, smart parcel lockers, and a pet washing station add everyday conveniences right at home, while boutique shops and restaurants just steps from your door connect you to the best of the neighbourhood.
