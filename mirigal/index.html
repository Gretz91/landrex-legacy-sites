About the Project

Background

Mirigal is a new neighbourhood proposed in Fort Saskatchewan’s recently annexed lands – it’s located in the southern portion near the river valley. This area presents a unique opportunity to create a pedestrian-friendly, intergenerational and connected neighbourhood, providing a diverse array of housing options and related services to support the growing population.

This page is an opportunity for you to see the proposed plans for Mirigal, understand how the land will develop, and be informed about the general timelines and the expected approval process.

We welcome you to share your feedback through the virtual survey, or by emailing our Project Team.

Planning Context

What are Area & Neighbourhood Structure Plans?

An Area Structure Plan (ASP) is a statutory planning document that guides future growth and development within a specific area of a city, while a Neighbourhood Structure Plan is a more detailed (non-statutory) plan that focuses on a specific neighbourhood within an ASP.

Both Plans Typically Establish
  • Land Use
  • Infrastructure and Servicing
  • Transportation Networks
  • Parks and Open Spaces
  • Development Phasing
Why are we creating the Mirigal NSP?

The Mirigal NSP provides a detailed framework for how this area will develop over time.

It builds on the broader vision established in the parent Pointe Aux Pins Area Structure Plan (ASP) and provides more detailed direction on orderly, efficient, and coordinated development of this future urban neighbourhood.

What work is being done?

Landrex Inc., a major landowner within the plan area, is leading the project. They have retained Arcadis Professional Services (Canada) Inc. to:

  • Prepare the Mirigal Neighbourhood Structure Plan (NSP)
  • Amend the existing Pointe Aux Pins Area Structure Plan (ASP)
  • Rezone the area for the first stage of development
