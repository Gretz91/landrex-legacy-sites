Homes For Sale in Northeast Edmonton

Prestigious Living at its Peak

At Ridgeview at the Quarry, residents can enjoy the tranquility of rural living while being just minutes away from a variety of amenities and services. With luxury homes nestled in a picturesque setting, this vibrant community offers the perfect blend of comfort and convenience for modern living.

Immerse yourself in a luxury lifestyle against the backdrop of not one but TWO premier golf courses. The scenic North Saskatchewan River valley also enhances the natural beauty of this one-of-a-kind community, and provides a serene escape for outdoor enthusiasts. Amidst this picturesque setting, residents enjoy a close-knit community atmosphere providing the ultimate peace of mind for themselves and their loved ones.

With convenient access to essential amenities such as top-rated schools, boutique shops, essential services, and a vibrant entertainment scene, residents enjoy a seamless blend of rural living and urban convenience. 

Ridgeview at the Quarry epitomizes luxurious living in a semi-rural setting, where every day offers a perfect balance of comfort, connection, and contemporary living at its finest.

More About Ridgeview at the Quarry

A Perfect Blend of Golfing, Nature, and Amenities

Ridgeview at the Quarry residents have access to a plethora of amenities ranging from top-notch schools and family-friendly services to shopping hubs and recreation venues, ensuring a convenient and enriching lifestyle for all. 

Boasting proximity to not only The Quarry golf course but also the nearby Raven Crest Golf and Country Club, golf enthusiasts will find themselves in paradise. In addition, the nearby river valley trail system and Northeast River Valley Park offers nature lovers ample opportunities to explore and enjoy the great outdoors. Ridgeview at the Quarry is a haven for both golfers and nature enthusiasts alike!
