The East Pine Townhome Collection

Welcome to East Pine, a vibrant new townhome community in southeast Beaumont, thoughtfully crafted for first-time buyers and growing families. This modern development features 42 beautifully designed townhomes, each offering three stories of open, contemporary living. Every unit includes a one or two-car garage, inviting outdoor piazzas, and plenty of lush green spaces to enjoy.

Two Exclusive Floor Plans to Suit Your Style

East Pine offers two exceptional floor plans, each tailored to reflect your unique preferences and lifestyle:

The Loft

An urban-inspired retreat, The Loft spans over 1,600 square feet with three bedrooms and two and a half bathrooms. Designed with an industrial flair, this model showcases distinctive details such as a den, mudroom, laundry room, exposed brick accents, metal finishes, and a striking blend of lighter upper and darker lower cabinetry.

The Brownstone

For those seeking timeless elegance, The Brownstone delivers sophistication and comfort. Spanning 1,100 square feet, this thoughtfully designed model includes two bedrooms, two and a half bathrooms, and a private primary suite. Highlights include polished gold fixtures, designer lighting, warm tones, and sleek white quartz countertops, all complemented by the convenience of upper-floor laundry.

The Community

East Pine is a joint venture between Landrex Inc. and the Fort McKay First Nation, located in the scenic southeast corner of Beaumont.

As one of Canada’s fastest-growing cities, Beaumont offers a unique charm, with its downtown core resembling a quaint French village. Its distinctive architectural touches, red brick walkways, and boutique-style shops create an inviting and picturesque atmosphere.

Beyond its charm, Beaumont provides a wide range of local amenities to meet your everyday needs, ensuring you rarely need to venture beyond the city limits. With top-rated schools, recreational facilities, and a thriving community spirit, it’s no wonder families and professionals are drawn to this vibrant city.

East Pine itself offers a variety of residential options, from estate homes to townhouses, all designed with modern living in mind. The neighborhood is surrounded by lush green spaces and scenic walking trails, perfect for nature enthusiasts and those looking for a peaceful retreat from the bustle of city life.

But what truly sets East Pine apart is its commitment to sustainability and community development. As a partnership between Landrex Inc. and the Fort McKay First Nation, we strive to create a harmonious balance between economic growth and environmental responsibility.

Through our sustainable building practices and initiatives, such as utilizing geothermal energy systems, rainwater collection, and solar panels, we are working towards reducing our carbon footprint and promoting a greener way of living. We also prioritize community engagement by hosting events and supporting local businesses to foster a strong sense of belonging among our residents.

At East Pine, you can enjoy the best of both worlds – modern convenience and eco-friendly living in a close-knit community. Come be a part of our vision for a brighter and more sustainable future at East Pine.
