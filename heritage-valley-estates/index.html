Embrace the Quiet Luxury of Heritage Valley Estates

Located at 106 Avenue and 104 Street, only 10 minutes from St. Albert, Heritage Valley Estates gives you the convenience of a quick commute but the quiet and privacy of a more peaceful community. Take a stroll around the beautiful, calming Fish and Game Pond, then head down to the lilac-lined walking paths, where butterflies are plentiful and the soothing scent of nature will wash away all your worries.

Don’t forget about the nearby fenced dog park, located just steps across from Heritage Valley Estates – where you can bring your pup to play with all of his neighborhood buddies.

Benefits of Living in Morinville

Morinville offers a small-town feel with everything you need close by. The town offers schools, well-maintained parks, local shops and restaurants, and recreational options – all within easy reach. Residents enjoy the comfort of seeing familiar faces, creating a strong sense of connection and belonging. Families value the safe environment, schools, and recreational facilities, while professionals benefit from the short commute to St. Albert and North Edmonton. It’s a quieter place to put down roots without giving up convenience.
