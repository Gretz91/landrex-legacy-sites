Located right next to Morinville’s brand new school, the St. Kateri Tekakwitha Academy, Westwinds is the perfect community for younger families.

Enjoy the convenience of having all of your favorite amenities only a short drive away while living in a thriving, family-friendly community with homes for sale in Morinville, Alberta that match your lifestyle and budget.

With your children in hand and the sunset casting everything in a warm, golden light, take a relaxing and rejuvenating stroll along the scenic pond. Why Should I Move to a New Home in Morinville?

Only 10 minutes north of St. Albert, Morinville is an up-and-coming and vibrant community.

Neighbors are always quick to smile and offer a gentle greeting, and you get the quiet and peace that’s not always possible in the big city. Plus, Morinville is filled with the type of charm you’ll only find in a close-knit community.

In addition, your budget goes further in Morinville. As land prices are more affordable, you can get Morinville homes for sale for less! Whether you’re a first-time buyer or looking to upgrade, homes for sale Morinville offer the space and quality you’re after—without the big city price tag.
