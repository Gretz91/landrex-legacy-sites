About the Community

As Fort Saskatchewan’s favourite family-friendly community, you’ll never run out of things to do!

Located near the river with 25 km of maintained walking trails, pristine green space, and both a playground and off-leash dog park, you have everything you need to keep the whole family busy—fur babies included!

There’s even a lookout point on the river, where you can watch the sunset with your family as you breathe in the scents of nature and feel the breeze brush across your cheeks. You might even see one of the adorable and friendly chipmunks that like to hang out here!

Plus, Windsor Pointe is conveniently located near Highway 21, making your commute to Sherwood Park or the city a breeze. With canola fields stretching as far as the eye can see and bathing the horizon in bright yellow, it’s both an easy and scenic drive.

Spend Less. Get More.

New homes in Fort Saskatchewan are more cost-effective than properties in Sherwood Park or Edmonton.

So you can finally have your dream home on a budget that works for you. No more pinching pennies or dipping into your vacation fund to purchase a home that’s perfect for your family. 

Featuring prominent builders and beautiful designs, our homes in Windsor Pointe are intended to impress. You can finally feel proud inviting friends and family over and hosting all the holiday events.

 

You’ll love spending quality time in these beautiful homes.

Plant your roots away from city life – Enjoy the time and space to take a breath. 

Explore the Outdoors

Enjoying nature is easy in Winsdor Pointe.

With nearby biking and walking trails that border the river, you can spend your weekends soaking up the sun and enjoying the beautiful summer days. 

Family First

Windsor Pointe puts families first

Featuring a nearby playground with a zipline, baseball fields, an outdoor basketball court, and nearby parks, there are plenty of opportunities for family fun—where you can create memories that’ll last a lifetime.
