Life on the Green Side

Tired of having to travel far from home just to get the essentials? Longing for a community that reminds you of the one you grew up in? Look no further than Fairways North in Stony Plain. 

Our new neighborhood is designed with your comfort in mind. At Fairways North, we offer a sense of comfort and community, where everything you need is right at your doorstep.Imagine having grocery stores, healthcare facilities, and entertainment options all within reach. Our vision is to create a place that feels like home, where neighbors become friends and every street corner holds fond memories.

Amenities at Your Doorstep

Welcome to Fairways North, a new community in Stony Plain that offers a slice of home right at your fingertips. With an array of nearby amenities such as shopping centers, restaurants, and parks, homeowners have everything they need within arms’ reach. 

Whether you’re looking to grab a bite to eat, enjoy a relaxing day at the park, or shop for groceries, you’ll never have to stray far from home to get the things you need. And for those who enjoy hitting the green, Fairways North overlooks a beautiful 18-hole public course, making it the perfect community for golf enthusiasts. Come experience the convenience and comfort of Fairways North, where every day feels like a hole in one!
